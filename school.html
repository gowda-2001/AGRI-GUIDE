Welcome to Neralekere

Rooted in values. Ready for tomorrow.

Sri Ranganatha Gramanthara High School provides meaningful secondary education for Classes 8, 9 and 10. Our school brings disciplined classroom teaching, digital learning, practical exploration, sport and caring guidance together so every learner can grow in knowledge, confidence and character.

President

Dr. Sri Sri Sri
Shivakumara Swamiji

Dr. Sree Sree Shivakumara Swamigalu founded the Sree Siddaganga Education Society in 1963 to provide accessible, high-quality modern and traditional education to all sectors of society. Known as Karnataka’s “Walking God” (Nadedaduva Devaru), he established this mission under the Sree Siddaganga Mutt in Tumakuru.

Vice President

Sree Sree Siddalinga Swamiji

Carrying forward the mission across more than 120 educational institutions in Karnataka, focused on rural growth and character building.

Vice President

Sree Shivasiddeshwara Swamiji

An M.Sc. graduate and former lecturer, anointed as successor pontiff of the historic Sree Siddaganga Math.

Secretary

Sri T. K. Nanjundappa

Supporting the school’s work in delivering strong, values-based education for the Neralekere community.

Our Purpose

Every child deserves a bright beginning.

Vision

To nurture responsible, knowledgeable and compassionate citizens through inclusive learning, strong values, curiosity, creativity and excellence in every part of school life.

Mission

To offer a safe, encouraging learning environment where experienced teachers combine clear fundamentals, modern resources and thoughtful guidance for every student.

Objectives

To build academic achievement, communication, digital literacy, health, sportsmanship and career awareness so students are ready for higher education and life.
